Customer Data Access Policy
Document ID: security-policy-001

CHUNK 8
External contractors are classified as third-party users.

CHUNK 24
Customer data is classified as restricted data.

CHUNK 51
All users, including third-party users, may download restricted data only to company-managed devices. This requirement is the device restriction.

CHUNK 79
An exception to the device restriction requires approval from the Security Director before the download occurs.
